<objective>
Make the chat router's absence a loud startup failure instead of a silent 404, and lock both that behavior and the `_DeferredMarketSource` pre-startup contract behind tests.

Purpose: Today an `ImportError` anywhere in the `app.llm.router` import chain (including transitively from `litellm`, a large fast-moving dependency) is swallowed as a warning and the app boots with no `/api/chat` at all — callers just get 404s. That "dev flexibility" is dead now that the LLM module ships with the app.
Output: `backend/app/main.py` raises on chat-router import failure; `backend/tests/api/test_app.py` covers the failure path, the mounted-route happy path, and every `_DeferredMarketSource` method's pre-startup `RuntimeError`.
</objective>
